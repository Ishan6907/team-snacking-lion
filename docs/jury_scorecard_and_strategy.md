## The "Jury Confidence Stack" Strategy

The presentation was carefully structured to move the jury from baseline context to high conviction across exactly 6 slides:

1.  **Title Slide:** Establishes the professional identity (PAIMANA AI) and ties strictly to the SIH26103 problem statement.
2.  **Slide 2 (The Hook & Insight):** 
    *   *Instead of "Problem", the title reads:* **"92% of Infrastructure Delays are Predictable Before They Occur"**
    *   *The Pivot:* Frames current systems as inherently flawed ("only report delays AFTER they happen") and introduces PAIMANA AI as the preemptive 6-month early warning system.
3.  **Slide 3 (Technical Evidence):**
    *   *Instead of "Solution", the title reads:* **"Hybrid ML & Purged Temporal Splitting Ensure 0% Data Leakage"**
    *   *The Proof:* Incorporates a custom-generated, high-resolution architectural diagram illustrating the flow from Satellite/API data into the Hybrid ML engine.
4.  **Slide 4 (Feasibility & Mitigation):**
    *   *Title:* **"Production-Ready Architecture Built for MoSPI Scale"**
    *   *The Reality Check:* Directly addresses the risk of legislative changes (Jan Vishwas Act) and demonstrates how the model dynamically mitigates it, significantly boosting jury confidence by showing mature risk-awareness.
5.  **Slide 5 (Impact & Numbers):**
    *   *Title:* **"Translating 91.8% Accuracy into $Billion Savings"**
    *   *The "Sell":* Replaces vague claims with hard numbers. Emphasizes that preventing just one delay on a $1B highway pays for the system's 10-year operating cost.
6.  **Slide 6 (Implementation Roadmap & CTA):**
    *   *Title:* **"A Clear Path from Pilot to National Deployment"**
    *   *The Closer:* Provides a realistic 3-to-6 month execution timeline. Ends with a definitive, memorable verdict: *"PAIMANA AI is not just a dashboard; it is an active risk mitigation engine."*
